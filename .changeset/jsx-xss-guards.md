JSX omits a `script` whose local name is `script`, including a prefix such as `x:script` and SVG `script`, when it has text or element children or any spelling of `src`, `href`, or `xlink:href`. A `link` whose `rel` or `relList` contains `stylesheet`, `preload`, `modulepreload`, or `import`, including an array or a `toString` value, is omitted. `preload` is omitted for every `as`. A reflected `rel` of any of those tokens drops `rel` and `href` after props apply. `prefetch`, `dns-prefetch`, `preconnect`, `prerender`, and `icon` keep an `https` href. A `text` prop is not applied on `script`. The renderer never assigns `__proto__`, `constructor`, or `prototype`, and it never assigns `host`, `hostname`, `protocol`, `port`, `pathname`, `origin`, `username`, `password`, `search`, or `hash`. Guarded URL attributes use the `safeUrl` allowlist (`http`, `https`, `mailto`, `tel`, and scheme-less strings that are not protocol-relative). That covers `srcset` and `imagesrcset` (HTML candidates), `poster`, `background`, `cite`, `manifest`, and `ping` (each token). `data:` is refused except a raster `data:image` (`png`, `jpeg`, `jpg`, `gif`, `webp`, `avif`) on `img` or `source` `src` and `srcset`. An `iframe`, `frame`, or `embed` `src`, and an `object` `data`, of `http:` or `https:` is not written. A non-string SVG paint value is dropped. `xlinkHref` is written as `xlink:href`. `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` is not applied.

Untrusted style, in any ASCII case of the prop and for a prefixed `style` element, drops `url(` (including a comment before the paren), `@import`, `image-set(`, `-webkit-image-set(`, `src(`, `expression(`, a `behavior` token, `-moz-binding`, and any declaration that contains `\`. The split on `;` and `}` ignores quotes and comments, so one dropped loader removes that rule and leaves a sibling rule. A semicolon is inserted between kept declarations and not after `}`. A sheet that is a single span containing a loader becomes empty. SVG `fill`, `stroke`, `filter`, `mask`, `clip-path`, `marker`, and `cursor`, and SMIL `from`, `to`, `by`, and `values`, drop a remote `url(` and keep `url(#id)`. `trustedStyle` keeps those loads and is a legal JSX child. `safeUrl` refuses `//host`, `\\host`, and `/\host`. `safeSpread` copies data keys such as `one` and `online`, drops real event-handler keys unless `handlers` names that exact key, and drops source-bearing keys such as `text` and `innerHTML`, plus `relList` and the URL property setters. A direct `onClick` function still registers.
