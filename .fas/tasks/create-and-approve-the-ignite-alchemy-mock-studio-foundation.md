# Create and approve the Ignite Alchemy component-specimen Mock Studio foundation and prototype

## Source
Created with `fas create-task` on 2026-07-21.

## Problem
Reframe Ignite Alchemy as a repo-scoped, Storybook-like component-specimen workbench. Its default executable review unit is a bounded component, feature projection, or explicitly labeled integration specimen backed by a fresh deterministic fixture, existing Ignite Story receipts, and an optional real machine or actor lens. Replace the broad Project Explorer and whole-application admission model with repository catalog -> specimen -> Story -> page -> admitted branch. Keep Voice Workbench honest as existing integration-specimen evidence rather than the default component taxonomy, and retain STORY-002 as the narrow source-backed golden path. MagicPath revision 9 remains donor-only evidence. The authorized specimen-first visual round produced browser-validated revision 11 at dreamily-sand-6842; human visual approval and all product-source implementation remain pending. Multi-project management, application generation, whole-app runtime connect or import, persistent registration, and broad capability negotiation remain deferred.


## Acceptance criteria
- The primary product hierarchy is repository/workspace container -> specimen catalog -> bounded specimen -> existing igniteTest Story -> ordered given/intent/behavior/checkpoint page -> admitted public-command branch.
- Boundedness is defined by authority and behavior seams rather than pixel dimensions; machines remain optional, shared app-owned actors may project through multiple specimens, and no machine-per-visual-atom rule is introduced.
- Voice Workbench is classified as an existing integration specimen and STORY-002 remains the narrow source-backed golden evidence without implying that a smaller production component already exists.
- ALCH-US-000 and the ALCH-NAR-000 root become a minimal repo-scoped catalog/open-specimen flow; create/connect/import and multi-project workflows are deferred from P0.
- ALCH-NAR-001 and its typed-fallback and retry-microphone branches remain the golden executable review path over real commands, machine truth, and receipts.
- MagicPath revision 9 is preserved only as donor evidence for shell geometry, docked Inspector, semantic status, and branch-lane layout; it is not approved specimen-first product behavior.
- The current Mock Studio narratives, authority matrix, experience inventory, design-system coverage, donor matrix, approval, provenance, and measurements artifacts are synchronized to the browser-validated specimen-first revision 11 candidate.
- Downstream POC, controller, lens, and host task wording is specimen-first; dependency edges stay unchanged and no dependency is added to dynamic semantic projection task-1783650880370 or retained-node task-1783719649309.
- Narrative readiness passes, foundation artifact review is approved, and the fresh specimen-first MagicPath revision 11 is published and browser-validated; revision 9 remains unapproved donor evidence and human approval of revision 11 remains pending.
- The work is tracked in `.fas/TASKS.md`.
- The task has a clear implementation and verification plan before execution starts.

Keep the existing Story receipt and real actor/machine authoritative while page, branch, replay, and optional-lens projections remain reviewer controls.
