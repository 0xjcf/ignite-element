# Produce the approved Ignite Alchemy MVP implementation handoff

## Source
Created with `fas create-task` on 2026-07-21.

## Problem
Reconcile the approved Ignite Alchemy MagicPath direction with the browser POC evidence and produce the behavior, boundary, state, token, responsive, accessibility, and parity contract that gates production MVP implementation. Preserve canonical Ignite and XState vocabulary underneath the scientific-alchemy product language. Record exact Story session and optional lens ownership, commands and controls, raw snapshots, derived views, evidence certainty, POC retain or rewrite dispositions, experience and design-system coverage, state-to-screen targets, implementation slices, and verification receipts. This is a handoff artifact, not production code or a public packaging decision.

## Acceptance criteria
- The handoff cites the approved MagicPath project, component and revision, measured prototype receipts, POC verification, and every retain, rewrite, or retire decision for POC source.
- Every visible control maps to a controller action, semantic command, navigation action, or explicit behavior-deferred disposition; no themed product term replaces the canonical technical contract.
- Story session lifecycle, fresh-fixture replay, optional XState observation, candidate-edge certainty, coverage provenance, raw and semantic evidence, and read-only CI or LLM consumption have single-owner boundary contracts.
- The final token, responsive, motion, focus, announcement, failure, no-lens, and accessibility rules define measurable parity targets for production implementation.
- The state-to-screen and narrative-to-experience matrices cover idle, running, paused, replaying, disposing, completed, failed, cancelled, unknown-cause, no-lens, excluded, and uncovered-gap states or record explicit dispositions.
- The handoff identifies the incremental production slices and exact verification evidence required for W3 through W8 without introducing a public package or Ignite API.
- The handoff labels the accepted product as an example-local MVP and defines second-adopter preview criteria without selecting a public package, CLI, hosted service, or compatibility guarantee.
- Structural validation proves every P0/P1 narrative, control, machine/lifecycle, product state, design-system need, and implementation slice has an approved, deferred, or blocked disposition with evidence.
- The work is tracked in `.fas/TASKS.md`.
- The task has a clear implementation and verification plan before execution starts.
- The task is queued in `.fas/queue/tasks.json` for the runtime.

## Proposed solution
- Reconcile the approved MagicPath source and measurements with POC behavior, evidence certainty, cleanup, browser, and verification findings.
- Produce one implementation handoff that maps narratives to authorities, session/lens facts, prepared views, controls, product surfaces, tokens, responsive states, accessibility rules, and parity targets.
- Convert every POC technique into a retain/rewrite/retire decision and every MVP requirement into a bounded W3-W8 implementation or verification obligation.

## Alternatives considered
- Rejected handing generated MagicPath React directly to implementation or treating POC success as production readiness.
- Rejected unresolved controls, implicit design-system gaps, unvalidated machines, and visual states without narrative or lifecycle provenance.
- Rejected packaging and public compatibility decisions before MVP dogfood and a second adopter.
